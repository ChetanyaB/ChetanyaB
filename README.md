## 👋 About me

<p align="justify">
I am an M.Tech student in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at Symbiosis Institute of Technology, Pune, with a B.E. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. I enjoy taking AI from an idea to a working product: preparing the data, training and evaluating the model, and shipping it behind an API or an interface that people can actually use.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Through internships at Dassault Systèmes, Yardi, DRDO and Hitachi Vantara, I have worked on agentic AI and knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation, document intelligence, hyperspectral imaging and predictive modelling. Outside work, I build complete AI projects of my own, and my deepfake voice detection system won the VOIS Innovation Marathon 2.0.
</p>
